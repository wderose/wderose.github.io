# Biography
I currently work on consumer experiences for PayPal [Shopping](http://paypal.com/shopping), [Giving](https://www.paypal.com/fundraiser/hub), and [Bill Pay](https://www.paypal.com/us/digital-wallet/manage-money/pay-bills). I previously built algorithms to power PayPal's [Marketing Solutions](https://www.paypal.com/us/webapps/mpp/paypal-marketing-solutions).

I was a PhD student of [Ken Goldberg](http://goldberg.berkeley.edu/) in UC Berkeley's [AUTOLab](http://autolab.berkeley.edu/) and [BAIR Lab](https://bair.berkeley.edu/). My research spanned topics including distributed cloud robotics and optimization for statistical learning.

I studied [math](https://www.pomona.edu/academics/departments/mathematics)
and [cs](https://www.pomona.edu/academics/departments/computer-science) at Pomona College and worked
on the [Analysis and Experimentation](http://exp-platform.com/) team at Microsoft as a software developer.
